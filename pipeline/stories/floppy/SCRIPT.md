  first sites Atlantic City, Nashville, Rochester + Atlanta and Salt Lake City hubs; 435 sites by end of 2028 (GAO's old

- Tom's Hardware, 2026-11-07: same facts; FAA still sources replacement parts from eBay; paper flight strips being

  https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/us-faa-to-eliminate-floppy-disks-from-air-traffic-control-by-2028-new-system-will-centralize-flight-alerts-restricted-airspace-schedules-and-pilot-reports
- General knowledge: a 3.5" HD floppy holds 1.44 MB (a phone photo is typically 2-5 MB); the floppy is the classic "save" icon;
  major manufacturers stopped making floppies (last big maker 2011). ATC runs 24/7, which is why systems are swapped live.
