# Firedog — robot dog in a fireproof coat walks out of a 1,000 °C fire

Run: 2026-11-28 02:00 Dubai (11-27 22:00 UTC). Category: **robots** (last robot story 2026-11-08 "tidy").

## Facts (event: live test 25 Nov 2026, Suzhou fire station; reported 26-27 Nov)
- Harbin Institute of Technology Suzhou Research Institute: quadruped robot dog wearing a fireproof "coat" ran 10 min in flames up to
  1,000 °C (1,832 °F) in a live test at a fire station in Suzhou, China.
- The coat is a fabric only ~5-6 mm thick; the same material flew on three Long March 5 rocket launches.
- Inside the coat the robot stays below ~80 °C (typical firefighting robots are rated to ~300 °C).
- It climbed stairs, located/reported the fire, and walked out on its own; laser imaging lets it see through smoke.
- Researcher Guo Shuai: "During the first test, all four feet were on fire when it came out." The rubber feet were reformulated.
