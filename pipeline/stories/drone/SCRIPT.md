# drone — Ring's flying home security camera finally launches, 6 years late, same price (2026-11-10)
Category: gadgets / smart home (rotation: robots 11-08, AI chips 11-09)

## Facts (event date: announced 2026-11-10; all articles 2026-11-10 unless noted)
- Amazon's Ring announced the Ring Drone Cam: indoor flying security camera, $249.99, US shipping 27 Jan 2027 (pending FCC). Engadget, BGR, Digital Trends, TechEBlog.
- History: Sept 2020 Ring showed the Always Home Cam, $249, "shipping next year" (TechCrunch 2020-09-24, TechRadar 2020-09-24). Late 2021 invite-only to a few US buyers, quietly gone by 2024, no general release (BGR). Siminoff: earlier models "just didn't work well enough" (Engadget).
- Setup: you carry it around the home so it builds a floor plan (BGR, TechEBlog). Suggests watch spots (doors, corridor ends).
- Waits in a dock, camera shuttered/off; takes off on command or when a Ring camera/sensor/alarm triggers (all sources).
- Propellers deliberately loud — a deterrent (BGR quoting The Verge "noisy as heck"; Digital Trends; TechEBlog).
- ~8 minutes of charging per 1 minute of flight (Engadget, TechEBlog). Flight 5-10 min (sources vary -> not stated).
- If Wi-Fi drops it lands / returns to dock (Engadget, TechEBlog).
