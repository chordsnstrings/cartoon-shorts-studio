## Facts (event dates checked in article bodies)
- ESA/JAXA BepiColombo was captured into orbit around Mercury on Saturday 21 Nov 2026; ESA confirmed capture Sunday 22 Nov (Deseret 2026-11-23; Gizmodo 2026-11-23; ESA arrival page: insertion scheduled 21 Nov).
- ESA (@BepiColombo, 22 Nov): "the thruster burn was shorter than planned due to a software issue. The spacecraft is safe" — ESOC investigating (Gizmodo; Deseret; Starlust 2026-11-23).
- A premature cutoff could leave it in a higher, less stable orbit than planned (Gizmodo) -> script says "may be parked higher than planned".
- Launched 20 Oct 2018 from Kourou (Starlust); ~8-year trip; flybys: 1 Earth, 2 Venus, 6 Mercury = 9 (Deseret; Starlust).
- The Sun's gravity speeds a craft up on the way in; it must shed speed to be captured by Mercury (Deseret; Starlust). Trip needed more energy than reaching Pluto (Deseret).
- Only the second spacecraft ever to orbit Mercury (first: NASA MESSENGER, 2011-2015) (Deseret; Starlust; Space.com headline "becomes 2nd ever").
- Stack: MPO (ESA) + Mio (JAXA); transfer module separated 3 Sep 2026; orbiters separate 9-10 Dec; science from April 2027 (ESA; Gizmodo).
