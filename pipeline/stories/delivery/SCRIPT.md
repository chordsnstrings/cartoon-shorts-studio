## Facts (event: Fri 20 Nov 2026 ~4:30 pm; FAA probe + neighbour video reported 23-24 Nov 2026)
- Huntington Woods, Michigan: an Amazon Prime Air MK30 drone (~80 lb, from the Hazel Park site) delivers a bottle of shampoo.
  The order notification said it would arrive by drone in about seven minutes (WXYZ 2026-11-21).
- Leaving after the drop-off it struck a fibre-optic cable (lost a propeller per a witness), hit a tree and came down on the
  neighbour's garage/shed roof, tangled in an electrical service line (FAA preliminary report via FOX 2 2026-11-24; WXYZ; CBS Detroit).
- DTE briefly de-energised the home; power restored. No injuries (CBS Detroit 2026-11-23; WXYZ).
- Buyer: "Like a sick bird, it tried to get out of here." (WXYZ). Prime Air team arrived ~10 min later; drone removed ~7:30 pm (WXYZ).
- Neighbour: Amazon will cover the damage, gave a $100 gift card (WXYZ). FAA investigating (FOX 2 2026-11-24; CNBC 2026-11-23).
- Amazon: "Safety is our top priority and incidents are extremely rare."
- Not used: buyer's/neighbour's names (private individuals), tree vs. cable order discrepancy beyond "clipped a cable".
