# catch — Starship upper stage caught by the launch tower (Flight 15)
Run: 2026-11-16 (UTC). Category: space-tech / rockets (last space story 2026-11-05; rotation after vehicles/gadgets/chips/robots).

## Facts (event dates checked in article bodies)
- Flight 15 lifted off from Starbase, Texas, Fri 13 Nov 2026, 5:47 pm ET (Sunday Guardian; Space.com).
- Rocket height: 41 stories (Space.com). 33 Raptor engines on the booster (Space.com).
- The ship (upper stage, Ship 42) reached orbit, deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, flew 14 orbits over ~22 h (Sunday Guardian; Space.com; TeslaNorth).
- Return Sat 14 Nov 2026: single-engine deorbit burn, swung over the Gulf, banked on final approach, three Raptors lit for the landing flip;
  the tower's "chopsticks" arms caught it ~22.5 h after launch — first time ever for the ship (TeslaNorth; Sunday Guardian; CNBC headline 2026-11-14 "SpaceX catches upper stage of Starship rocket in historic first"; Hackaday Links 2026-11-15).
- SpaceX post: "Mechazilla catches Starship!" (TeslaNorth).
- Booster 22 (the part caught before) aborted its catch: a low-fuel-mass alarm cut its boostback burn short; it diverted offshore and exploded just above the Gulf of Mexico ~6.5 min after liftoff (Space.com 2026-11-14; Sunday Guardian 2026-11-15).
