# kei — Toyota's new US pickup tops out at 25 mph (2026-11-11)
Category: vehicles / gadgets (rotation: robots 11-08, AI chips 11-09, smart-home gadgets 11-10)

## Facts (event: reveal events 2026-11-09 in Ypsilanti Twp MI + Frisco TX; press release + coverage 2026-11-10/11)
- Toyota revives the Scion brand in the US as "Scion by Toyota"; first product the KEI-01 kei mini pickup (Toyota press release 2026-11-10; Jalopnik, Autoblog 2026-11-10; Gizmodo 2026-11-11).
- Top speed 25 mph, built to the federal low-speed vehicle rules (FMVSS 500), which are less strict than for normal cars/trucks (no same crash standards) (press release; Gizmodo).
- State/local law decides where it can go; in many states roads with limits of 35 mph or less (Gizmodo; Autoblog "figuring out a legal route might not even be possible for your commute").
- Just over 11 feet long, under 5 feet wide; roughly half the length of some full-size American pickups (press release; Gizmodo).
- Right-hand drive, 660cc three-cylinder, CVT, selectable four-wheel drive, 600 lb bed, three-way fold-down bed, heat and A/C (press release; Gizmodo; Jalopnik).
- Assembled at Toyota Indiana from H2 2027, $34M, ~50 jobs; fleet pilot first, retail maybe later (press release; Autoblog).
- Scion launched in the US 2002, discontinued 2016 (Autoblog).
- Rebadged Daihatsu Hijet / Toyota Pixis (Jalopnik). Not used in the script.
