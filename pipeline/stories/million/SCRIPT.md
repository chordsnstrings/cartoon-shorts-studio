## Facts (event: FCC decision Wednesday 25 Nov 2026)
- The FCC gave SpaceX conditional approval to deploy up to 1,000,000 "Starmind" satellites: solar-powered AI computing
  satellites linked by laser to each other and to Starlink (TechCrunch 2026-11-25; Gizmodo 2026-11-25; CNBC 2026-11-25 headline;
  Fox Business 2026-11-25 headline).
- SpaceX has "just over 11,000" Starlink satellites in orbit; Starmind would raise the total number of satellites in Earth orbit
  "roughly 50-fold" (Gizmodo).
- Schedule: first operational satellite by Nov 2033, 10% by 2035, 50% by 2038, all by 2040 (Gizmodo; TechCrunch: 7 / 14 years).
- Orbits 500-1,002 km (TechCrunch). Launch on Starship (Gizmodo).
- The American Astronomical Society warned of interference with ground-based astronomy, debris and congestion (TechCrunch, Gizmodo);
  the FCC said several concerns, incl. threats to scientific research, fell outside its regulatory jurisdiction (TechCrunch, Gizmodo).
- NOT approved: the waiver to send retired satellites above 600 km to graveyard orbits (some even around the Sun) instead of
  re-entering - the FCC said SpaceX "had not provided sufficient information about its proposed disposal process" (Gizmodo, TechCrunch).
- Objections from Amazon ("lofty ambition rather than a real plan") and Viasat (TechCrunch) - not used.
